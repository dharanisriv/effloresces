🌸 Effloresces

«Bloom. Arrange. Express.»

Effloresces is a 3D interactive bouquet-making and botanical exploration web application that combines immersive virtual garden environments, flower discovery, flower symbolism, personalized bouquet creation, and digital greeting cards.

The project provides users with a virtual garden experience where they can explore themed flower roads, discover flowers and their meanings, create customized bouquets, write personal messages, and generate elegant greeting cards.

---

🌷 Project Overview

Traditional online flower-gifting platforms mainly rely on static catalogs and predefined bouquets. Effloresces provides a more interactive and personalized experience.

Users can:

- 🌿 Explore an immersive virtual botanical garden
- 🌸 Discover different flowers through interactive exploration
- 💐 Learn about flower meanings and symbolism
- 🎨 Create personalized bouquets
- 💌 Add custom messages to bouquets
- 🎴 Generate digital greeting cards
- 📤 Export and share their creations

The application combines 3D environments, interactive animations, realistic flower models, and personalized content to create an engaging digital flower experience.

✨ Key Features

🌺 Interactive Virtual Garden

Explore a visually immersive 3D garden environment with multiple themed roads and locations.

🛤️ 8 Themed Garden Roads

Each road represents a different flower theme:

Road| Theme
🌸 Pink Road| Love & Gratitude
🌼 Yellow Road| Joy & Friendship
🌹 Red Road| Love & Passion
🤍 White Road| Purity & Grace
💙 Blue Road| Peace & Wisdom
💜 Purple Road| Royalty & Creativity
🌿 Green Road| Harmony & Renewal
🧡 Orange Road| Energy & Warmth

🔎 Interactive Flower Discovery

Users can explore flowers in the garden and interact with them to discover information about their symbolism and meanings.

🌹 Flower Meaning Cards

Each flower provides information such as:

- Flower name
- Symbolic meaning
- Emotional significance
- Relationship or occasion relevance

💐 Personalized Bouquet Builder

Users can select flowers from the garden and add them to their own customized bouquet.

💌 Custom Messages

Users can write personalized messages to accompany their bouquets and greeting cards.

🎴 Greeting Card Generator

Create elegant digital greeting cards using the selected bouquet and custom message.

🖼️ High-Resolution Export

Completed creations can be exported as high-resolution PNG images for sharing or saving.

🔐 User Authentication

The application includes user authentication for managing personalized experiences.
